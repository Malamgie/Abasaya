Late Alhaji Musa Ibrahim

The Central Ancestor

Historical Biography

A Legacy of Integrity, Service,
& Enduring Character

Late Alhaji Musa Ibrahim came to live with his uncle, Late Alhaji Malam Ghana, as a teenager in the year 1946. After spending about a year under his uncle’s care and guidance, his uncle assisted him in securing employment at R/Zayam.

While working at R/Zayam, he married his first wife and began building his profound family life. Subsequently, he was transferred to Bara town in the Kirfi Local Government Area, where his character, exceptional dedication, and integrity earned him great respect within the community.

In recognition of his exemplary conduct and service, the Emir of Bara conferred on him the prestigious traditional title of First Dalatun Bara.

During his stay in Bara, he married other wives and was blessed with more than twenty children, though a few of them later passed away. Late Alhaji Musa Ibrahim was widely known as a man of integrity, honesty, and strong moral values. He lived a life worthy of emulation, leaving behind a legacy of respect and good character that continues to guide our family today.

He fell ill in the year 1982 and, after a period of sickness, passed away in 1985.

"May Allah (SWT) forgive his shortcomings and grant him Al-Jannatul Firdaus. Ameen."
