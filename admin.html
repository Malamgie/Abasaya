System Integrity Policy

As an administrator of the Abasaya digital archive, you are bound by the strict data preservation rules of this platform.

  • Family members cannot be deleted to prevent fracturing the hierarchical tree.
  • Unverified entities marked with "(?)" must remain until official confirmation.
  • Duplicate names should not be merged unless confirmed as the same individual.
  • You may update biographies, portrait URLs, and status (Living/Late) via the People Directory.
