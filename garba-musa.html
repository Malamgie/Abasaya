The First Son

Garba Musa

"A life dedicated to national service, local leadership, and the enduring strength of family."

G

Born in the serene and historically rich village of Bara, located in the Kirfi Local Government Area of Bauchi State, Garba Musa stands as the venerable first son of Late Alhaji Musa Ibrahim (Abasaya). From his early years, he exhibited the steadfast character and sense of responsibility expected of a family's firstborn, carrying the mantle of leadership that would define both his private life and his illustrious public career.

Garba Musa’s professional journey is a testament to national dedication and administrative excellence. As a distinguished civil servant, he built a formidable career within the National Electric Power Authority (NEPA, which later transitioned into PHCN). His expertise and leadership in the power sector saw him traverse the nation, serving with distinction in the historic city of Maiduguri, navigating the bustling economic hub of Lagos, and eventually guiding operations from the nation's capital, Abuja.

His ascent through the ranks was marked by integrity and a profound commitment to public service, ultimately reaching the pinnacle of his career as an Executive Director at NELMCO.

However, the height of corporate success did not detach him from his roots. Following his well-deserved retirement, Garba Musa answered the call of his people, returning to his hometown. He channeled his decades of executive experience into grassroots development, serving honorably as the Chairman of the Kirfi Local Government. His tenure brought seasoned leadership back to the very village of Bara where his story began.

Beyond his public accolades, Garba Musa is a patriarch in his own right. Blessed with four wives, he has cultivated a massive, thriving branch of the Abasaya family tree. With twenty-four living children and a rapidly growing generation of grandchildren, his legacy is not just etched in government records, but alive in the bustling, united family he continues to lead today.
